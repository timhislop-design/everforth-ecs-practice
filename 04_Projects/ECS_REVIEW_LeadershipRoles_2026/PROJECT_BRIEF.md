## Working Rules — How to Work in This Folder

- **This is an internal organizational-design review**, not a solicitation response. No compliance matrix applies.
- **The V4 redline is the working document.** All edits carry Word tracked changes with Timothy Hislop as the revision author; keep track changes ON for any future edit pass.
- **Decision-rights anchors (do not contradict in any revision):**
  - Delivery Lead = single point of accountability for all of delivery; PMO Lead and Lead Architect report to this role.
  - Deliverability gate: no bid/proposal/PCR/extension submitted without the Delivery Lead's certification.
  - Two-key pricing: Delivery Lead owns pricing strategy + hours/LOE/staffing mix; Practice Lead approves final rates + margin. A price rejected on margin is fixed via rates/margin — never by cutting hours unilaterally.
  - Practice Lead = business owner (strategy, P&L, rates/margin) + independent trust-but-verify oversight of delivery health.
  - No dotted lines from LA/PMO to the Practice Lead — the Delivery Lead already reports to the Practice Lead, so independent visibility runs through the governance cadence (health reviews, gate outcomes, red-account reviews), not a second reporting line. (Tim's correction 2026-07-16.)
  - Framing rule for all revisions: balanced — every role owns its domain outright; accountability shared but never ambiguous. Position Delivery Lead gains as system design (authority matching accountability), paired with matching obligations (gate cuts both ways; owes leads clear priorities/protected decision rights).
- **Self-contained:** all deliverables stay in this folder. Never modify OOTB collateral (00–03).
- **Branding:** `.docx` via `EcsDocument` (`ecs_template.py`) for new documents; the redline preserves the source document's own formatting.
