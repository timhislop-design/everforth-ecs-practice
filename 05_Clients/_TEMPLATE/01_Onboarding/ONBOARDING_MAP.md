# [CLIENT] Onboarding Map — Curation Framework

> Template. Fill in per engagement. Roles, layering, and the client cut are starting points — adjust to the engagement.

## The Principle

The OOTB library (00–03) is the full body of practice knowledge. An engagement uses a **curated subset**, not the whole thing. Two failure modes to avoid:

1. **Team overwhelm** — handing every consultant the entire library. Fix: each role gets a short, sequenced reading path; everything else is reference.
2. **Client overwhelm** — sending the client internal depth and every guide at once. Fix: a small, staged, plain-language subset tied to what they need to *do* next.

**Curate, don't copy.** Pull from the library by role and by moment; reference the rest.
